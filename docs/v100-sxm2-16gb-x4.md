# NVIDIA V100-SXM2 16GB x4 — layer split vs tensor parallelism

Measured 2026-09-14 to 09-15. Methodology **METHOD v1.2**
([`../METHOD.md`](../METHOD.md)).
Raw data [`../results/v100-sxm2-16gb-x4.jsonl`](../results/v100-sxm2-16gb-x4.jsonl).
Derived metrics live in
[`v100-sxm2-16gb-x4-metrics.md`](v100-sxm2-16gb-x4-metrics.md).

> Measurement conditions are in METHOD. This document holds only this platform's
> environment, results and interpretation.
> Conclusions that span platforms are in [`../FINDINGS.md`](../FINDINGS.md).

**These are SXM2 modules adapted to PCIe.** Power delivery and cooling differ from
factory PCIe V100s, and **there is no NVLink.** They went into the same motherboard as
the P104-100 cards and were measured **with the same binary**, so the difference
between the two reports comes from the cards.

**The goal is to find out which of the P104 conclusions depend on the card.** The two
platforms differ by roughly 16x in interconnect bandwidth, 2x in VRAM and about 5.7x
in compute. As a result **the single-card baseline and the `layer / MTP on / 2 cards`
cell — both impossible on P104 — became measurable here**, and both the sign of the
MTP result and the tensor scaling efficiency changed.

**PCIe link** — `Gen3 x16 is the maximum`. This is a hardware ceiling, not an idle
downshift, and it is the same under load.

```
index, pcie.link.gen.current, pcie.link.gen.max, pcie.link.width.current, pcie.link.width.max
0, 3, 3, 16, 16
1, 3, 3, 16, 16
2, 3, 3, 16, 16
3, 3, 3, 16, 16
```

**Topology** — raw `nvidia-smi topo -m` output. **Identical to P104-100** (same
motherboard), which removes topology as a variable.

```
        GPU0    GPU1    GPU2    GPU3    CPU Affinity    NUMA Affinity   GPU NUMA ID
GPU0     X      NODE    NODE    NODE    0-15            0               N/A
GPU1    NODE     X      NODE    NODE    0-15            0               N/A
GPU2    NODE    NODE     X      PHB     0-15            0               N/A
GPU3    NODE    NODE    PHB      X      0-15            0               N/A

  NODE = Connection traversing PCIe as well as the interconnect between
         PCIe Host Bridges within a NUMA node
  PHB  = Connection traversing PCIe as well as a PCIe Host Bridge (typically the CPU)
```

No NVLink and no PCIe switch. Only GPU2–GPU3 cross a single host bridge (PHB); every
other pair crosses several (NODE).

## 2. Results

- **Prefill (pp)** = how fast the prompt is read. `prompt_per_second`
- **Generation (tg)** = final output token rate. `predicted_per_second`
  (confirmed output, not drafts — `predicted_n = 400` on every turn)
- Cold is one run, warm is the mean of four turns
- **Percentages** are relative to **the slowest cell for that metric**, which is 100.
  The three baselines differ

| Metric | Baseline cell (100%) | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Cold pp | tensor / MTP off / **1 card** | 723.9 t/s |
| Warm pp | layer / MTP on / 3 cards | 376.6 t/s |
| tg | layer / MTP off / 4 cards | 27.29 t/s |

### 2-1. Generation speed ranking (13 measured cells)

| Rank | Cell | tg | % |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **tensor / MTP off / 4 cards** | **46.62** | **171%** |
| 2 | tensor / MTP off / 3 cards | 42.79 | 157% |
| 3 | tensor / MTP off / 2 cards | 41.64 | 153% |
| 4 | tensor / MTP on / 2 cards | 39.17 | 143% |
| 5 | tensor / MTP on / 3 cards | 37.60 | 138% |
| 6 | layer / MTP on / 2 cards | 29.43 | 108% |
| 7 | layer / MTP on / 3 cards | 28.72 | 105% |
| 8 | layer / MTP on / 4 cards | 28.25 | 103% |
| 9 | layer / MTP off / 2 cards | 27.97 | 102% |
| 10 | layer / MTP off / 3 cards | 27.78 | 102% |
| 11 | layer / MTP off / **1 card** | 27.71 | 102% |
| 12 | tensor / MTP off / **1 card** | 27.45 | 101% |
| 13 | layer / MTP off / 4 cards | 27.29 | **100%** |
| — | tensor / MTP on / 4 cards | **failed** | — |

**Three things to read off this.**

1. **The five tensor cells own the top of the table.** The slowest tensor condition
   (37.60) is 28% faster than the fastest layer condition (29.43).
2. **Every layer cell, from one card to four, lands between 27.29 and 29.43.** Four
   cards or one makes no difference.
3. **All three `layer / MTP on` cells beat all three `layer / MTP off` cells.**
   **The opposite of P104**, where the MTP-on layer cells were last.

**The cold pp ranking disagrees with the tg ranking.** The cold leader
(`layer / MTP off / 4 cards`, 215%) is **thirteenth on generation (100%)**. Choose a
configuration from one metric and you pay for it on the other.

### 2-2. Layer split, MTP off

| N | Cold pp | % | Warm pp | % | **tg** | **%** | Warm turn |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | **1,555.5** | **215%** | 487.6 | 129% | 27.29 | **100%** | 16.2s |
| 3 | 1,435.3 | 198% | 492.7 | 131% | 27.78 | 102% | 15.9s |
| 2 | 1,091.1 | 151% | 490.9 | 130% | 27.97 | 102% | 15.9s |
| **1** | 747.4 | 103% | 488.4 | 130% | **27.71** | 102% | 16.0s |

**N=1 is measured.** The 13,115 MiB model fits on one 16GB card (METHOD 1-6), so
`tg_1` in the derived metrics is not a derived value.

### 2-3. Layer split, MTP on

| N | Cold pp | % | Warm pp | % | **tg** | **%** | MTP acceptance | Warm turn |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 1,211.0 | 167% | 376.8 | 100% | 28.25 | 103% | **32.0%** | 16.2s |
| 3 | 1,131.5 | 156% | **376.6** | **100%** | 28.72 | 105% | **32.0%** | 16.0s |
| 2 | 918.3 | 127% | 379.4 | 101% | **29.43** | **108%** | **32.0%** | 15.6s |

**All three cells accept at exactly 32.0%.** The draft and accepted counts are also
identical — `2440 / 780` summed over four warm turns — reproducing METHOD 6-2:
**layer split is deterministic regardless of N.**

**The `layer / MTP on / 2 cards` cell that failed on P104 completes here.** There, two
8GB cards could not take the MTP draft context and hit an OOM. **The extra VRAM is
what made the difference.**

### 2-4. Tensor parallelism, MTP off

| N | Cold pp | % | Warm pp | % | **tg** | **%** | Warm turn |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **4** | 887.4 | 123% | 594.1 | 158% | **46.62** | **171%** | **9.9s** |
| 3 | 961.3 | 133% | 612.0 | 163% | 42.79 | 157% | 10.6s |
| 2 | 1,140.5 | 158% | **677.1** | **180%** | 41.64 | 153% | 10.8s |
| **1** | 723.9 | **100%** | 461.5 | 123% | 27.45 | 101% | 16.2s |

**At one card, layer gives 27.71 and tensor gives 27.45 — 0.97% apart.** The split
mode does effectively nothing on a single card. The tensor figure being lower is
**presumed to come from running second on a warmer card**, but that was not verified.

### 2-5. Tensor parallelism, MTP on

| N | Cold pp | % | Warm pp | % | **tg** | **%** | MTP acceptance | Warm turn |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 800.3 | 111% | — | — | **failed** | — | — | **Xid 79** |
| 3 | 736.4 | 102% | 398.7 | 106% | 37.60 | 138% | 29.2% | 12.6s |
| 2 | 1,040.7 | 144% | 469.2 | 125% | 39.17 | 143% | 27.8% | 11.9s |

**The four-card cell completed its cold turn and died on the first warm turn.** Cold
prefill was 800.3 t/s and cold generation **37.73 t/s**, almost identical to the
three-card cell at 37.60 — **it was producing normal figures right up until it died.**
There was no performance degradation as a warning sign. With no four-turn warm mean,
the `tg` column is left empty.

**The 3 and 2 card figures are re-measurements.** The original run happened
immediately after the `tensor-mtp-4` crash and was contaminated (section 7).

Acceptance differs by cell (29.2% / 27.8%). **Tensor parallelism changes the generated
content as N changes** (METHOD 6-2), so this difference must not be read as an effect
of GPU count.

### 2-6. Temperature

**Throttle data could not be aggregated.** The harness in use was METHOD v1.1, whose
telemetry query lacks `clocks_throttle_reasons.active`. **Only temperatures survive.**

| Cell | Peak | Mean |
|---|---|---|
| layer-nomtp 4 / 3 / 2 | 54 / 61 / 67 | 48.0 / 50.5 / 49.5 |
| layer-mtp 4 / 3 / 2 | 62 / 62 / 67 | 51.7 / 50.8 / 49.4 |
| tensor-nomtp 4 / 3 / 2 | 62 / 66 / **76** | 54.8 / 56.2 / 55.0 |
| **tensor-mtp-4 (crashed)** | **50** | **42.1** |

**A 76 °C peak, well clear of the V100 protection threshold of roughly 85 °C.** Tensor
mode runs about 5 °C warmer on average because every card works at once.

⚠️ Temperatures for `tensor-mtp` at 3 and 2 cards were **overwritten by the
re-measurement** and are omitted.

## 3. Interpretation

Figures and calculations for the derived metrics (session crossover, scaling
efficiency, MTP batch economics, roofline) are in
[`v100-sxm2-16gb-x4-metrics.md`](v100-sxm2-16gb-x4-metrics.md). Conclusions that span
platforms are in [`../FINDINGS.md`](../FINDINGS.md).

### 3-1. Layer split does not use the cards during decode here either

```
1 card 27.71  →  2 cards 27.97  →  3 cards 27.78  →  4 cards 27.29
```

**With a measured single card this is no longer an inference.** Four cards deliver what
one does, the same pattern as P104's 8.08 → 7.97 → 7.82.

Prefill is different — cold pp rises from 747.4 to 1,555.5, a **2.08x** gain, while
warm pp sits at 488.4 / 490.9 / 492.7 / 487.6, **completely flat in N.**

### 3-2. Tensor parallelism scales with card count — on both platforms

```
2 cards 41.64  →  3 cards 42.79  →  4 cards 46.62      +12.0%
```

P104 rose too, 13.06 → 14.14 → 17.89, by **+37.0%**. **The relative gain is in fact
larger on P104.** The efficiency comparison is in the metrics document and
[FINDINGS 5](../FINDINGS.md).

### 3-3. Cold favours layer, warm favours tensor — the direction holds

```
cold   layer 1,555.5  vs  tensor 887.4    =  layer 1.75x
warm   tensor 594.1   vs  layer 487.6     =  tensor 1.22x
```

P104 gave 1.91x and 1.51x. **Same direction, smaller magnitude.**

### 3-4. MTP turned into a gain in layer mode

```
P104   layer 4 cards   7.82 → 6.54    −16.4%   loss
V100   layer 4 cards  27.29 → 28.25    +3.5%   gain
```

**Acceptance barely moved, 29.9% → 32.0%.** What changed is the cost multiple of batch
verification; the calculation is in the metrics document and the mechanism in
[FINDINGS 4](../FINDINGS.md).

**In tensor mode it is still a loss** — −12.1% at three cards, −5.9% at two. **On the
same card the sign depends on the split mode.**

## 4. MTP is a variance problem more than an average problem

Warm 4-turn `tg` spread.

| Cell | Warm 4 turns | Spread |
|---|---|---|
| layer / MTP off / 4 | 27.75 / 26.53 / 27.71 / 27.19 | 4.6% |
| layer / MTP on / 4 | 26.61 / 27.26 / 31.02 / 28.10 | **16.6%** |
| layer / MTP on / 2 | 28.06 / 27.80 / 31.78 / 30.09 | **14.3%** |
| tensor / MTP off / 4 | 46.48 / 46.62 / 46.63 / 46.74 | **0.6%** |
| tensor / MTP on / 3 | 38.72 / 36.19 / 35.61 / 39.87 | **12.0%** |
| tensor / MTP on / 2 | 35.77 / 39.95 / 40.53 / 40.41 | **13.3%** |

**The MTP-off tensor cell is the steadiest at 0.6%, while every MTP-on cell swings
12~17%.** Per METHOD section 4 the 5% rule does not apply to MTP-on cells; **the
spread is reported as a result.**

Even `layer / MTP on`, where the average is a gain, swings 14~17%. **Erratic response
time makes the perceived quality worse than the average suggests.**

## 5. Xid 79 — `tensor + MTP + 4 cards`

**It died both times.** The first was during the 12-cell matrix run; the second was a
repeat of that single cell with the power limit lowered to 250W. The second time GPUs
0, 2 and 3 were lost together. Both were fully recovered by a reboot.

```
NVRM: Xid (PCI:0000:01:00): 79, GPU has fallen off the bus.
```

**The other three four-card cells all completed** (`layer-nomtp-4`, `layer-mtp-4`,
`tensor-nomtp-4`). **The same combination completed on P104 at 14.82 t/s.**

Causes ruled out: overheating (50 °C peak in the crashing cell), sustained power (60%
of the cap, and it reproduced at 250W), ECC (zero retired pages) and a single faulty
card (three lost at once). The remaining hypothesis is **PCIe transaction frequency**,
and it is **undetermined.** The full account and the evidence are in
[FINDINGS 7](../FINDINGS.md).

**In practice it is simply avoidable** — the fastest configuration,
`tensor-nomtp-4` at 46.62 t/s, has no MTP, and MTP is a loss in tensor mode anyway
(2-5).

## 6. Limits specific to this platform

1. **`tensor-mtp-4` is permanently missing.** Do not extrapolate the tensor-mode MTP
   result to four cards
2. **Thermal and power throttling could not be aggregated.** Measured with the v1.1
   harness. Only temperatures survive, and re-measuring would mean running the
   crashing cell again, so it was not done
3. **`tensor-mtp` at 3 and 2 cards are re-measurements.** The originals were
   contaminated (section 7). Both values are preserved in the raw data
4. **These cards are SXM2 modules adapted to PCIe.** Power delivery and cooling differ
   from factory PCIe V100s and there is no NVLink. Results may not carry over to stock
   cards
5. **Why layer and tensor differ by 0.97% at one card was not established.** It is
   presumed to be a temperature difference from run order
6. **No Q6_K comparison was run.** The quantization conclusions in the P104 report are
   not confirmed on this platform

| | Cold pp | 884.2 | **1,040.7** |

**`tensor-mtp-3` was badly contaminated** (tg +10.6%, prefill +30%).

**Both the contaminated originals and the re-measurements are published.** The reader
has to be able to check the basis for the judgement. They are also a worked example of
a crash leaving its mark on subsequent cells (METHOD section 4).

Every line in the raw data carries a **`validity` field**, so the distinction is
visible from the file alone. A contaminated line and the line that replaces it sit
**adjacent in the file**, so the values can be compared directly.

| `validity` | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `ok` | Normal |
| `failed` | Produced no result |
| `contaminated` | Measured right after a crash. **Superseded but kept as evidence** |
| `remeasured` | The replacement for a contaminated cell |

Each line also carries a `note` field giving the basis for that verdict.
